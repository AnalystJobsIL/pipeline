dispatch and EMPTY on a schedule (the falsy-true-branch trap is documented on the line).

the runner read the flag and print what they would do — `deliver --dry-run` (existing),
`mark_sent` (`WOULD mark sent from …`), `persist_state.py commit --dry-run` (NEW: every gate

by path, then unstaged; the audit line is not appended), `publish` (`WOULD publish
docs/index.html (N bytes)` + the optional files), `outcome` (records the verdict into the
checkout, no `--commit`), `notify_relay` (`WOULD hand the relay …`). It cannot collide with
the scheduled run, which is exactly what the 08-30 manual dispatch did. **It spends what a
real run spends**: Bright Data under `BD_RUN_CAP`, the subscription token, ~30 runner
