`tests/rehearse_roles.py`. Backlog: `docs/BACKLOG.md` 132–141 (note: 132/133 are numbered

## What was wrong (all verified against the committed store, 2026-08-25)

- `cloud_state/seen.db` `matched`: 111 rows / 90 companies / 35 `sent`. **Three job urls
  sat under two company names each** — Armis + OTORIO (same `seen_id`, OTORIO parked
  `alias-of Armis` on 08-23 but its row stood, in the archive under the wrong name);
  Meta + Meta Israel (same `seen_id`; the url is the listing page); Port + Port.io
  (**different** seen_ids `comeet:15.F68` vs `scrape:<url>`, same url, the scrape title
  `Senior BI Analyst Tel Aviv - Israel`; both rows ACTIVE → the posting was on the board
  twice). The full dry run also found HP + HP Indigo. `companies.csv` has 13 active groups
  reading one board under two identities (BACKLOG 133); `scraped_cache.json` holds 16 urls
  under two company keys.
- Nothing at fetch, classify or store time compared posting identity across companies:
  `job["company"]` is the registry row's name (`fetchers.py`, 14 sites;
  `scrape_universal.py:485`); `merge_key` includes the company; `seen_id` does not.
- The role record was a committed 1.4 MB sqlite binary: not diffable, not mergeable, no
  closure/repost/tags/verdict, `first_seen` reset erased history, `jd_attempted` added by
  another lane's `ALTER TABLE`.
- BACKLOG 124: a role on two boards was classified twice and the bare copy could win.

## Rejected alternatives

- An append-only event log (needs a replay; unbounded; the conflict path is `cp -rT` so
  there is no merge to benefit from). A single JSON dict (one bad byte = the file; one giant
  line-diff a day). Descriptions inline (kills the diff). The ledger as the only store (four
  other lanes' tools read `matched` by SQL — BACKLOG 132 is the path).
- A url-host vs `api_url`-host check per job: every job url is on the ATS host, and
  `company_identity.is_foreign` is `False` on every ATS host by design (BACKLOG 21).
  Posting identity (`seen_id`, then url + title prefix) is the only free, exact signal.
- Treating the claim winner as an attribution judgement. It is a dedupe with stability;
  for Broadcom/VMware-class pairs both names are real and the registry decides (133).

## Defects found by the rehearsal before any reviewer saw it

1. `reconcile` took the earliest `first_seen` from either side — undoing sqlite's
   deliberate >3-day reset (the rule the email re-alerts on). Now sqlite's wins whenever the
   row exists; the ledger keeps the older opening in `episodes`.
2. A rehydrated row carried the literal status `open` into sqlite; the next morning's
   reconcile let it out-vote the ledger's `closed` (clobber case: `reopened 0`, dangling
   `closed_on`). sqlite now holds exactly one status, `superseded`.
3. A reopen reported `merged: 108` — every row rewritten because NULL ≠ `open`; fixed by
   comparing in the ledger's shape and never writing open/closed to sqlite.
4. The rehearsal's first version ran `run()` unscoped and overwrote the real `docs/*.html`,
   `stale.json` and `health_baseline.json` (restored from git). Every rehearsal is scoped now
   — and so must any local "full" dry run be: an unscoped `pipeline.run --db scratch` still
   writes those four files (§0's "never touch the real seen-store" is about the store only).
5. The mass-close fixture could not trip a floor of 10 with 5 roles; the rehearsal lowers
   `MASS_CLOSE_MIN` to 2 and empties two consecutive days (`_alive` gives one day's grace).
