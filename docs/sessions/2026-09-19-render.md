somebody else* and *who may BE NAMED by one* — out of a single `tokens` dict, and the filter
that made a name safe to be named also made that company unable to speak for itself.

## What changed — one function, two dicts

`pipeline/rolecard.py:cross_check`, block (c), and nothing else:

- `_toks(key)` — the ≥4-character identity tokens, or the whole key when there are none. The
  derivation was written inline; it now has one name and four call sites.
- `victims` — today's rule (`_COMMON_WORDS`, single token under 5 characters) **plus
  `len(key.split()) > 1`**: a two-word brand is not its second noun. That was 4 of the 6
  (`Air Products`→`products`, `Poc System`→`system`, `Capital One`→`capital`,
  `REE Automotive`→`automotive`), and `Google Israel`→`google` is the same shape one step
  earlier, because `_ID_SUFFIX` strips the trailing `israel`.
- `mine` — every card that has a company name, by **every spelling that company writes**: its
  key's tokens, the tokens of every `ALIASES` key that folds into its identity
  (`doit`→`doitintl`), and the tokens of `display_name` **read off the CARD**.
- the accuser filter `me not in tokens` is **gone**: `mine` covers every named card, so a
  company that cannot be a victim is still an accuser.

Two deliberate choices inside that:

1. **The brand comes from the card, never from the firmographics record.** The card's value
   has already passed `display_name()`'s impersonation guard, and block (b) has already
   revoked it where two employers collided — so the 2026-08-30 contract's scope (the guard
   sees the render dict) is unchanged, and a record carrying another employer's name cannot
   buy that employer an excuse.
2. **`ALIASES` is read, not written.** Nothing in `firmographics.py` moved, so no
   `company-intel` state changes.

## What the plan (and `632`) got wrong, and the measurement that says so

- **`632`'s diff verbatim breaks a pinned test.** Its (b) removes a two-word brand from
  `tokens` altogether, and because the me-check reads `me not in tokens`, the same removal
  silences that company as an accuser. `Oak - Identity` (key `oak identity`, tokens
  `['identity']`) stops accusing and
  `test_cross_check_names_five_wrong_company_shapes_and_only_those` — which has pinned
  `blurb-names-other Oak - Identity→Sckipio` since 2026-08-30 — fails. It also silences
  `בנק דיסקונט`, hiding a real duplicate: **1 hit where the split design measures 2.**
- **`632`'s "Check" line is not achievable and should not have been.** It asks for
  `blurb-names-other` **0** times over board+archive; the union prints **1**. That is not a
  failure of the fix: `בנק דיסקונט` has 1 archive card and 0 board cards, `Discount Bank` has
  1 board card and 0 archive cards, each product cross-checks its OWN population, and **a
  pair no single product sees whole is a pair no product alarms about**. (`Pagaya` has no card
  at all — that pair lives only in the cache.) So there are three honest numbers, and a report
  must say which: `--blurbs` (the cache, 2), `--cards` (the union, 1), the mail (0).
- **The second survivor changed identity.** 632 predicted `הראל…→Harel Insurance & Finance`;
  after `Air Products` stops being nameable, `בנק דיסקונט`'s first-match becomes its own
  Latin twin instead.
- **614(a) measured and left open.** 2 of 289 ledger records carry a mangled title, both
  pipe-only: `ONE datAI …` (closed) and `Bank Leumi … 3103` (withdrawn). Split over today's
  surfaces: board **0** hidden, email **0**, archive **2** (a withdrawn row is still a
  `matched` row, so it renders in the archive). A render-only "a short skills tail is not a
  blob" rule would therefore unhide **2 archive cards and 0 open/board/email roles** while
  changing what the board hides for every future pipe. The value is in the CANON agreeing
  with the gate, which is `roles`'s file. Filed under 614 with those numbers.

## Rejected, each with the number

- **632's diff as written** — the pinned `Oak` assertion fails and 1 < 2 on real duplicates.
- **A write-time refusal of such a blurb** (the 09-18 prompt's idea) — 6 hits, **0**
  impersonations: it would refuse 6 correct blurbs to prevent none. The defect was the reader.
- **Adding `products`, `system`, `capital`, `automotive` to `_COMMON_WORDS`** — the class is
  structural, not lexical: 4 of 6 hits, and the next two-word brand would need a new word.
  The test asserts these four are NOT in `_COMMON_WORDS`, so a word-list patch cannot pass
  for this fix.
- **Deriving the brand from the firmographics record** — that value has not passed
  `display_name()`'s guard; the card's has.
- **A threshold check in `--blurbs`** (`FAIL if hits > 2`) — a pinned count "broke within a
  day and took the registry gate down with it" (`docs/sessions/2026-08-30-docs-ci.md`). The
  mode prints the pairs and fails only on an EMPTY cache, which is a broken measurement.

## Residue, named on purpose

Three of the six hits were a **Hebrew-keyed row with an English blurb** (`מטריקס`,
`קבוצת כלמוביל`, `בנק דיסקונט`). They are silent now only because their victims became
unnameable: the accuser half still cannot find its own name in its own text, since no
spelling `identity_key` knows transliterates `מטריקס` into `Matrix`. The class returns the
day such a blurb names a single-token English company. **The cheap fix is not render's**: a
`display_name` or an `ALIASES` entry for that row makes the blurb self-excusing today, which
is exactly the mechanism this change added — noted for `company-intel` under 632.
