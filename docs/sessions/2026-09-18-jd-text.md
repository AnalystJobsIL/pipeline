## What is harder for the next lane

* **`strip_head` now takes a second argument.** `whole=` is the PAGE the body was cut out of,
  and `mirrored_nav` cannot see the menu without it (`jd_body` has already deleted the footer
  copy that identifies it). A new caller that composes `strip_head(jd_body(x))` and forgets
  `whole=x` silently loses the head cut. The two callers that matter pass it.
* **`jd_body` is no longer a pure marker scan.** It tokenises the text (`mirrored_nav`) on every
  call, and `looks_like_jd` calls it for every row at every reader. Nothing measurable came of
  it here (2,421 bodies in a few seconds), but a future caller in a tight loop should know.
* **A `failed:` cell exists and is not a blocker.** Anything that reads `jd_why` must decide
  between three prefixes now, not two, and `_STAMP_FREE` is the one place that says which of
  them a writer may take.
* **`_store_text` has a `canonical` argument and it is load-bearing.** Passing `canonical=True`
  from a donor path would make the defective-text arm a way to install a sibling's posting.
* **The Waze row's stub still sits in the store**, failing the bar on purpose. It is held off
  the board by the publish gate, not deleted — deleting a text is not this layer's move.
