1. **The caps counted markup.** Hebrew served as `&#x5E0;` is eight characters a letter; 11 of 29
   headings fit `{5,140}`. `_UNIT` counts an entity as one character in all three card patterns,
   with DISJOINT alternatives: an entity alternative beside `[^<]` backtracks exponentially on an
   over-cap heading (1.9 s at 20 entities, ×3.6 per two more; the design agent's 200-entity run
   never returned). 29 over-cap headings now parse in 0.001 s.
2. **Decoded, the group was still refused.** All 29 matched and the role-ratio gate asked `ROLE`,
   which is English: 3 of 29 against a floor of 9. The 11 that survived on the served page passed
   by arithmetic luck (3 ≥ max(2, 11//3)). `_ROLE_HE` adds bounded Hebrew stems (`(?<![א-ת])…(?![א-ת])`,
   so `תפקיד`/`מרכז`/`מחשב` are not `פקיד`/`רכז`/`חשב`).
3. **The wrong city was a substring.** `אילת` sat inside `שאילתות` ("queries") in the description;
   `ISRAEL_LOC`'s lookarounds guarded Latin letters only. It now has Hebrew right edges; on the left,
   a one- or two-letter proclitic (`בירושלים`) and a word-final letter (`מיקור חוץראש העין`, fused
   card text) still read. Two of Cal's cards were also Azor, from `אזור הצפון` ("the north
   region"): `_noun_reading` returns the region phrase whole, skips any other noun use, and treats
   `מודיעין <word>` as intelligence unless the word is `עילית`. Over the whole cache: **0** stored
   locations stop passing `ISRAEL_LOC`, **86** cards' own-text reading moves, all read.
4. **The refresh had no row vouch.** `_page_is_il(url, page_html, vouched=False)` now has two
   halves: a row vouch (the hunts' env flag, or `assume_il` from the refresh) AND an Israeli place

   HOST under `.il`, never the path or query. Cal's page token is `בישראל` in its about-us text.

posting. So:

  (`hebrew and any(hrefs)` → skip); DOM admission still asks the anchor's whole text for `ROLE`;
- the host vouch alone assumes **only an address-less card** (`add.vouched_only`); the hunts' env
  flag keeps its old reach, pinned by the test;

  `.co.il` board lists `AI-First Engineering`, `Growth & Learning` as a heading group, and 6 such
  bullets would have been admitted.
