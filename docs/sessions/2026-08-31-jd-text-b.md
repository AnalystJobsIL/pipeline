## What shipped

`_donor_pass` in `enrich_matched_jd.py`, over four donor classes bound to the ROLE:
`own-address` (a link on the employer's own listing page — `jdfill.role_addresses_on`),
`cache` (a card whose `store.merge_key` IS this row's), `copy` (a LinkedIn/Indeed copy this
role's `seen_ids` name — `jdfill.source_copy_url`), `archive` (Wayback of the role's own url).
It reaches the rows `run_backfill` never walks, which is where three of the six were. The
design and its rejected alternatives are in
`docs/decisions/2026-08-31-cross-source-sibling.md`; the model is §7a.

**Enumeration is generous; admission is not.** The three structural classes identify
themselves; a `copy` is fetched at somebody else's address and is admitted only when the
fetched document DECLARES ITSELF to be this title at this employer (`declared_identity` →
`doc_names_role`). Not `names_in_url` — `store._same_origin` refuses that by measurement —
and not byte-similarity, which is the fanout symptom (`370`), not an identity.

**`matched.jd_why`** records `ok:<class>:<host>` / `refused:identity(N)` /
`structural:<reason>(donors:N)` per row: `443`'s jd-text half, closed on the matched store.
The `roles` lane reads `structural:` verbatim into the published `description_blocker`
column — contract agreed live with that session (their names: `description_blocker`,
meta `description_text.blocked`; `ok:`/`refused:` are never blockers; a row whose text passes
`looks_like_jd` never carries one). **They build the ledger carry, so I filed no diff for it.**

**Two bugs that made half this board unreachable by construction**, both found while chasing
G Stat and both fixed here:

* `_SLUG_WORD` was `[a-z0-9]+`, so every slug rule in `jdfill` answered False for a Hebrew
  posting. Measured over all 4,379 cached (url, title) pairs: `is_job_url` changes on **0**,
  `title_in_slug` gains exactly **3**.
* `urllib` raises `UnicodeEncodeError` on a non-ASCII URL before a packet leaves, and
  `plain_fetch`'s catch-all turned that into the same silent `(None, "")` a timeout gives —
  so a Hebrew address was unfetchable AND indistinguishable from a network failure.
  `wire_url` fixes it: over the 3,815 distinct URLs in both caches, 3,812 are ASCII and **0**
  change; the 3 that change are the ones nothing could fetch before.

**`JDFILL_INDEED_CAP` 8 → 25.** The first night the rung ran, the digest logged `the Indeed
cap bound at 8 — 20 Indeed postings judged on their snippet tonight`: 28 wanted it, 8 got it,
and Oak and Diageo were emailed with 172 characters as their description. 25 × 30 = **750 a
month, 15 %** of the September pool, inside the shared `JDFILL_BD_CAP` of 30 that the whole
inline layer spent 12 of that night.

## What three adversarial Opus waves found — and one thing they made me delete

Waves A (identity laundering), B (money and fake evidence) and C (vacuity and interlocks) ran
against the diff. **Wave A got text from a different role and a different employer past the
gate; wave B got a published lie out of a run that read nothing.** Every finding below was
reproduced before it was fixed, and each carries a test:

* **The employer half was payable by the TITLE.** A declaration always contains the title's
  words, so `Bright Data` was confirmed by the `data` in `Data Analyst` — the exact pair
  `store._same_origin` cites. Four live rows stood in that shape. The company must now be
  named by a word the title does not supply (`c_only = c_words - t_words`), and legal forms
  (`ltd`, `inc`, `co`, …) name nobody — my own refusal test caught `M Co` matching `Other Co`.
* **`own-address` admitted a bigger opening.** The title rule was a subset test, so
  `Data Analyst` matched `senior-data-analyst-growth-2042` — and `own-address` is structural,
  so no document check follows it. Measured: 15 distinct different-title adoptions in the
  cache. Symmetric now, and an ambiguous match on EITHER path yields nothing (the id path had
  no guard, and `scrape:36` is a live id: `/blog/36` won over `/careers/position/36`).
* **A donor could overwrite a role's own posting.** The pass also walks rows the LLM tier
  called incomplete — those pass `looks_like_jd` — and `_store_text`'s ratchet is a LENGTH
  ratchet between two JDs. A donor may now only fill a row that has no description.
* **`structural:` was written on evidence nobody collected**: a listing page that returned
  503, a failed CDX lookup, a capped or disarmed Unlocker, an unreadable ledger. Four
  published blockers came out of a run with every socket shut. It now requires the
  enumeration to have COMPLETED, and `wayback_snapshot` returns `None` (lookup failed) as
  distinct from `""` (the archive has none).
* **`structural:` outlived the text that disproved it** — the cache rung and `_reclean` write
  descriptions and never touched `jd_why`. Cleared at `_store_text`, the one choke point.
* **The pass ignored `--limit`, the 7/14/28 cooldown and the clock.** `--limit 1` worked three
  rows and spent four credits. It now shares the operator's budget, has its own 4-minute
  budget, and — one condition closing three holes — the paid rung obeys `due()`, so a row
  stamped yesterday is not re-bought and a `gone` row never spends at all.
* Smaller: one malformed `href` killed the whole pass (`Invalid IPv6 URL`); `matched_structural`
  counted superseded rows; the seen_ids re-parse dropped `sibling_urls`' lossy-column guard;
  `cache_by_merge_key` crashed on a non-string title in another lane's file; refusals were not
  counted when a later donor filled the row — which suppressed exactly the alarm worth having.
* **A host gate could be walked past.** `wire_url` nameprep-encodes the host on the way to the
  socket, so `il.inde<soft-hyphen>ed.com` was a host `paid_only` had never heard of and the
  wire turned into `il.indeed.com`. `_host_of` now normalises identically.

**Deleted rather than shipped: a head-chrome trim** (`skip to main content`). I added it
mid-session on a measurement of "24 texts cut, 0 damaged". Wave A could not reproduce the 24
(it measured 13), showed a constructed posting whose own subject is that phrase losing its
first sentence, and showed the two rows I named as beneficiaries could not be reached — net
live effect **0 rows**. Line-anchoring it dropped the corpus effect to 2. A change whose
stated number does not reproduce is the failure this repo punishes hardest, so it is out, with
its corrected measurement, rather than in with a footnote.

Two wave findings are ACCEPTED, not fixed, and say so here: `alarm_for` still cannot see the
donor pass (it can report `jd-massfail` on a night donors filled four roles — the same
pre-existing blind spot `from_cache` has), and `--dry-run` now makes free network requests it
did not before (it builds no Unlocker and writes no `jd_why`, but a rehearsal log prints
`structural:` lines that are not written).
