So today's answer shape is a **302 whose `Location` names the capture**, after 18 s or after
minutes, with 520s in between — and the plan's first two conclusions each lasted an hour:
"treat a 15 s timeout as pending" (request 2 seemed to refute it) became "hold 150 s"
(request 5 refuted that), and the shipped design is a 60 s wait with a timeout recorded
`pending`, verified next run through `archive.org/wayback/available` (answered in a second
where CDX took six and once returned nothing). Three threads share one 6-s pace gate so
the 15/min per-IP limit holds whatever the thread count.

The daily cap was left at the operator's 100, and the stamp carries `backlog` (4,603 on day
one) so the operator can raise `WAYBACK_DAY_CAP` / `WAYBACK_WORKERS` on a number rather

## 3b. What the adversarial wave found (one Opus pass over the diff, 20 findings)

Fixed before the push, each with a test: **retries were unreachable** (`fresh + retry`
under a cap that fresh alone exceeds every day — a fifth of the day is now reserved for
them); **the verify loop ran outside the pace gate** (~20 requests a minute against the
archive's 15); **`pending` was an absorbing state** (a failing lookup never wrote
`unverified`, and the set was sorted by url so the alphabetical tail was never asked —
oldest-first and bounded now); **three workers collapsed on one 429** (the second thread
through the lock ended the day before anyone paused — one episode now); **`canon()`
corrupted percent-encoded queries** (`%25` → `%`; the raw pairs are filtered now);
**a multi-memento `Link` header recorded its oldest capture** (newest now); **two tests
busy-spun for 137 s** under a stubbed sleep (the gate reserves and sleeps once). Corrected
in the docs: the "weekly" board rotation is a 3-week lap at 25 a day over 532 pages; the
"~six weeks" drain ignored an inflow of ~124 a day that exceeds the cap; "5 commits" is 7;
the LinkedIn share of the whole target set is 46.5 %, not 88 % (that was the discovery
cache alone); the jd-archive banner said no step was `continue-on-error`. Left as found:
`guard_kill`'s 12 KILLS are vacuous for a new module (every test errors at import when it
is reverted) and no `tests/mutations.json` record was added; a `cached` answer that names
an old capture is recorded with that old `snap`, which is the truth.
