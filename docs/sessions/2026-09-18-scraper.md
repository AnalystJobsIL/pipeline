## 1. The address (`b36a653`) — byte proximity answered with the neighbour

`_card_href` took the LAST href before the heading when it was byte-nearer than the one
after. On a row layout — `…<a href=PREV>svg</a></span></div><div class=position-row><span
class=position-row-title>T</span>…<a href=OWN>` — the previous card's CLOSED anchor sits
~140 bytes before the heading and the card's own link ~400 after, so distance answers with
the neighbour. `_card_anchor_for` cannot rescue it on Deloitte, Camtek or 888 because their
slugs are ids (`df-070-en`, `AF.059`, `?uid=7A.D6F`): no anchor text, no `ROLE` word. The
after-search also ran to `pos + 1600`, past the next sibling.

`_card_bounds(page_html, pos, end)` gives one card a range. It begins at the anchor that
WRAPS the heading — one still open at `pos`, the `<a href><h3>` shape that declares its own
address (434) — and ends at the next sibling heading, which `_from_cards` already computed as
`ends`. `end` has no default, so an unbounded window cannot come back.

**One defect my own first version created.** `lo` was the `href=` attribute's position, not
the `<a`'s. `_card_anchor_for` reads that window with `_ANCHOR_RX`, which needs the whole
element, so a window starting inside the open tag silently lost the address: Centrical's
three `<a href=own class=single-job><h3>T</h3></a>` cards fell back to the listing. The
replay is what found it; `_A_OPEN` fixed it, and a test asserts `_card_bounds`'s `lo` is the
`<a`.

## 2. The carry (`9485ae3`) — the listing is not a name

20 cards across 9 boards held a sibling's description. `_carry_jd` keys its address fallback
by `_addresses(j)` = `[url, job_id]`, and a url-less card's `url` is the LISTING that every
url-less card on the board shares (`_Adder.__call__`). So `prev[listing]` was whichever old
card came last, and its text went to any NEW title with an empty description. Git shows the
spread one night at a time: Medison's compensation body sat on `Senior Total Rewards Analyst`
alone from 2026-08-31 and held five titles by 09-16.

`_addresses(j, listing)` drops the url when `_is_own_address` says the card does not own it.
An unknown listing still means "cannot tell" and keeps it. The id stays and is the honest
key: `_Adder` hashes `company|title|location` into a url-less card's `job_id`, so it names
the card and not the board.

**`carried_twins=N` on the `collect:` stamp** — url-less cards holding one body under two
titles, counted over what the run WRITES, not over the event, so a re-introduction shows up
whatever produced it. Question 6, answered by grep: the mail renders the whole collect stamp
in its `Stage order:` line (`digests/latest.md` line 53, `pipeline/digest.py:302`), beside
`legacy_loc=9 ownless=1194`. No new channel.

## 3. The place (`ba674d6`) — anchored at the `<title>` copy, which is byte 0

`_parse_position_page` read `at = txt.find(title)`, and the first occurrence of a title in a
page's text is the `<title>` element's copy at the top of the document. "The place nearest the
role's name" was therefore the place nearest byte 0 — whatever chrome lies between `<title>`
and the `<h1>`. אסם's navbar slogan (`אסם נסטלה יוצרת עתיד בשדרות`, at offset 210 of a
2,724-character capture) placed 10 of its 12 postings in שדרות; the real place sits at 1,395,
right after the h1 (`משרה מספר 414885 Petah Tikva, IL, 4951729`).

The anchor is now the on-page `<h1>`, mapped from its position in `body` into the same
tag-stripped text. A page with no h1 keeps the old anchor — the `og:title` branch lives in
the head, where the anchor would be 0 anyway. `_H1_RX` / `_OG_TITLE_RX` are named once and
serve both the title and the anchor.

`_bare_country`: the country alone is the absence of a place dressed as one. Anchoring at the
h1 made that reachable — `<title>DevOps Engineer in Ramat Gan, Israel</title>` over a bare h1
puts `Israel` nearer the heading than the city — so a reading that yields only the country
defers to the role's own CLAIM (heading + document title), which `_loc_from_ctx` will only
ever answer with an Israeli match (a Braga title still yields ""). It also catches the
`Israel Israel` that `_LOC_SUFFIX` reads back off a page whose `<title>` is "Israel", which
my first version shipped on three pages.

**Proved live, 2 credits.** `https://www.osem-nestle.co.il/career/open-positions/414885`
→ `שדרות` becomes **Petah Tikva**; `…/419068` → `שדרות` becomes **Modiin**. Both pages answer
plain HTTP with 403.

**Replayed over 452 real position pages** (baseline tree vs this one, identical bytes):

`il` or 0 change `foreign`.** Gains: Spectrum Dynamics ×4 `Israel`→`Caesarea`, OncoHost

**Rejected on the same replay**: stripping `<nav>/<header>/<footer>/<aside>` from the search

follows, and that is the rule that keeps a place the page prints above its heading — the
anchor moved, the reading did not become chrome-blind. Every אסם page prints its own city
after the h1, so all 12 are fixed anyway.
